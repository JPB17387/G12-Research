Appendix A

Proposed Intervention Program

Goal: To encourage students to use their smartphones more sparingly, implement controlled screen time behaviors, and convince parents to monitor online activities.

Core Objectives

  • Time Regulations: Limit phone usage during lectures and study blocks.
  • Age Awareness: Guide younger students to understand that phone dependency hurts study habits.
  • Disciplinary Measures: Apply self-imposed screen time rules to decrease addiction likelihood.

Timeframe Guidelines

  • Ages 0 - 13: Free internet access should be avoided. Devices should only be used for direct family calls, parent-approved learning apps, or short, timed games.
  • Ages 13 - 15: Screen restrictions can be moderately relaxed (e.g., 1 to 3 hours daily limit), using search engines with safe-search filters and parental controls.
  • Ages 15 - 18/21: More control should be handed over to foster personal responsibility. Screen limits may be lifted as academic assignments require unrestricted search tools and long research blocks.
